Same question, same answer. The only difference is where the notes live. The old way phones a faraway computer (about 2,000 ms — a long, awkward "…hello?"). Moss keeps the notes right next to the brain, so a lookup is a thought, not a phone call — about 6 ms, no pause. That "no waiting, and it just knew" feeling is the whole hackathon.
